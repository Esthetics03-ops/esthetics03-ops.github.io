Chrononutrition 101: Timing is Vitality

Aligning your metabolic clocks with MindSync Biometrics.

In the world of holistic wellness, we often obsess over what we eat. But according to the latest research in Chrononutrition, your body’s internal clock cares more about the timing of your intake than the ingredients themselves.

1. The Gut-Clock Connection

Every organ has a "peripheral clock." Eating late at night wakes up your liver and pancreas while your brain is trying to shut down for repair.

MindSync Insight: Eating within 3 hours of sleep drops Heart Rate Variability (HRV) by up to 20%, ruining recovery.

2. The 12-Hour Reset

To synchronize gut health, ensure at least 12 hours between your last meal and your first meal the next day. This triggers autophagy (cellular cleanup).
