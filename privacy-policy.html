Privacy Policy

Your privacy is fundamental to our high-performance wellness ecosystem. This policy explains how we handle data at The Esthetics Hub.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide a better experience, including:

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, and technical metrics via MindSync AI.
  • Cookies: Small data files used to remember your preferences.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We use third-party advertising companies, specifically Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website.

  • Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. Log Files

The Esthetics Hub follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. This includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), and date/time stamps.

4. Data Protection (GDPR/CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have the right to access, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. External Links

Our site contains links to other websites (Affiliates, Research Papers). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites.
