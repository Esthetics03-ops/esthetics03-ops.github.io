Why stress matters

Short summary about stress physiology and impact on cognition.

Evidence-based techniques

  • Box breathing — 4-4-4-4 breaths to activate calm response.
  • Progressive muscle relaxation
  • Mindful walking
  • Sleep hygiene

HOW TO: Daily practice (10 minutes)

  1. Find a quiet place.
  2. Sit comfortably and set a 10-minute timer.
  3. Start with 2 minutes of deep belly breathing.
  4. Do 3 rounds of box breathing (4s inhale, 4s hold, 4s exhale, 4s hold).
  5. Finish with a short body scan for tension.
