How Biometric Syncing Works

MindSync doesn't just track data; it interprets your body's "silent language." By analyzing three core biometric pillars, our AI creates a real-time emotional map.

  • HRV Analysis: We measure the variation in time between each heartbeat to determine your Autonomic Nervous System (ANS) balance.
  • Dermal Response: Tracking subtle changes in skin conductivity to detect early-onset stress spikes.
  • Circadian Alignment: Syncing your recovery cycles with your daily activity to prevent burnout.
