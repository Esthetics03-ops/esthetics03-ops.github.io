Top 10 Nutrition Tips

  1. Eat whole foods — choose minimally processed items.
  2. Prioritise vegetables — aim for a variety of colors.
  3. Balance macronutrients — protein, fat, carbs each matter.
  4. Stay hydrated — water supports digestion and cognition.
  5. Include healthy fats — nuts, seeds, oily fish.
  6. Limit added sugars — reduce sweetened drinks and snacks.
  7. Watch portion sizes — use plates and mindful eating.
  8. Plan meals — simple meal prep beats last-minute junk.
  9. Mindful eating — chew, pause, enjoy.
  10. Consistent patterns — regular meals and sleep improve metabolism.

HOW TO: Apply these tips (7-day starter plan)

Follow this weekly plan to build habits.

Day 1–2: Audit & swap

Track current meals, swap one ultra-processed item for a whole-food alternative.

Day 3–4: Veg boost

Add an extra vegetable to each meal; try new colors.

Day 5: Hydration focus

Carry a water bottle and aim for 2–3 liters (adjust for activity).

Day 6: Protein check

Ensure each meal contains a protein source—plant or animal.

Day 7: Plan & reflect

Create a simple 3-day meal plan and reflect on changes.
