Why Nutrition Matters

Good nutrition fuels more than just muscle; it regulates your hormones, sharpens cognitive function, and strengthens your immune response. In a world of ultra-processed convenience, returning to basics is the ultimate "biohack."

Top 10 Nutrition Tips

  • 1. Eat Whole Foods: Prioritize items with single-word ingredients. (Try: Organic Nut Butters)
  • 2. Prioritize Vegetables: Aim for the "Plate Method"—50% of your plate should be greens.
  • 3. Balance Macronutrients: Every meal needs a clean protein, a healthy fat, and a complex carb. Need a boost? Check out Impact Whey Protein.
  • 4. Strategic Hydration: Drink 500ml of water immediately upon waking to kickstart metabolism. Invest in a BPA-Free Motivational Water Bottle.
  • 5. Include Healthy Fats: Omega-3s are vital for brain health.
  • 6. Limit Added Sugars: Swap soda for sparkling water or herbal teas.
  • 7. Portion Control: Use smaller plates to trick the brain into feeling full.
  • 8. Smart Meal Prep: Preparation is the enemy of temptation. We recommend these Glass Meal Prep Containers.
  • 9. Mindful Eating: Turn off the TV. Focus on the texture and flavor of your food.
  • 10. Consistency: One "bad" meal won't ruin you, just as one "good" meal won't fix you.

HOW TO: The 7-Day Nutrition Kickstart

Day 1–2: The Pantry Audit

Remove "Trigger Foods" (high-sugar/processed). Replace them with raw almonds or walnuts.

Day 3–4: The Color Challenge

Eat at least three different colors of vegetables per meal.

Day 5: Hydration Mastery

Add electrolytes to your water if you're active. (Try Liquid I.V.)

Day 6–7: Batch Cooking

Prepare 3 days of lunches in advance to avoid the "Friday Takeout" trap.
